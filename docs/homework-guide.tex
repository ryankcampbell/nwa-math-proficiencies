\guidetitle{Before December 2: Your Homework}{NWA Mathematics \quad due Wednesday, November 25}

The December 2 meeting starts from \textbf{results}, not from a blank page. Before then, open the
\textbf{Workshop} (link in Ryan's email; sign in with your \textbf{@nwacademy.org} account) and go to
\ui{Review}. The \textbf{My homework} box at the top keeps your score. It takes about 30 minutes,
and you can do it in pieces.

\begin{multicols}{2}
\section*{1. Update your course}
\ui{Courses} $\to$ your course. Read every proficiency. Fix the wording, move it to the right thread,
retire it or add what's missing (Teacher's Guide, section~1). When you've read the whole course, click
\ui{Mark as read through} at the top of the page. Edits count from October~21.

\section*{2. Vote on every card}
In \ui{Review}, every possible overlap, every open question and every proposal has five buttons.
\textbf{One click is a vote}; it saves at once.
\begin{itemize}
\item \ui{Keep both}: different depth, so both stay
\item \ui{Move to one course}: it belongs in only one
\item \ui{Merge wording}: same idea, so make the statements match
\item \ui{Add a proficiency}: something is missing
\item \ui{Needs discussion}: bring it to the meeting
\end{itemize}
A one-line \textbf{reason} box appears once you vote (optional; press Enter to save). Changed your
mind? Click another button. Click your vote again to take it back. \ui{Next card to vote on} jumps to
the next card you haven't voted on.

After you vote on a card, you see how everyone has voted on it so far. Ryan sees who voted what.
Votes stay in the department Sheet and are never published.

\columnbreak
\section*{3. Propose what's missing}
See a \textbf{hole} (no course teaches it), a \textbf{collision} (two courses teach the same thing) or
anything else the department should decide? \ui{+ Propose an item}: pick the type, tick the courses,
and write a line or two. It goes straight onto everyone's ballot. You can withdraw your own proposal.

\section*{Done means}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Votes cast} shows \emph{X of X}
\item your courses are \textbf{read through}
\item any holes you know about are \textbf{proposed}
\end{itemize}
Voting closes at 11:59\,pm on \textbf{Wednesday, November~25}. On November~26 the tallies go into the
meeting agenda, so on December~2 we start with the cards everyone agrees on and spend the time on the
ones marked \emph{Needs discussion}.

\section*{Good to know}
\begin{itemize}
\item Votes are advice, not decisions. Spiral, redundant or resolved is recorded at the meeting.
\item New proposals appear for others after they press \ui{refresh} in the teal bar.
\item Something not working? Tell Ryan.
\end{itemize}
