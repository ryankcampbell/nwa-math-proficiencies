\guidetitle{Scope \& Sequence: Teacher's Guide}{NWA Mathematics \quad 2026--27}

\textbf{Public site} (share freely): \href{https://ryankcampbell.github.io/nwa-math-proficiencies/}{ryankcampbell.github.io/nwa-math-proficiencies}
\\[3pt]\textbf{Workshop} (editing; link from Ryan): \rule{3.2in}{0.4pt}

The Workshop is the public site with edit buttons. Sign in with your nwacademy.org Google account.
Everything you do saves immediately, with your name on it. Nothing goes public until Ryan clicks
\ui{Publish}, so experiment freely. Every proficiency has a permanent code, like \texttt{IM3-14}.

\begin{multicols}{2}
\section*{1. Sort your course (fall, Round 1)}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \ui{Courses} $\to$ your course. Switch the view to \ui{By strand}.
\item For each proficiency, check the \emph{thread} it sits in (e.g.\ ``Exponential \& logarithmic
  functions''). Wrong home? Pick a new one from its drop-down.
\item \ui{Edit wording} to fix a statement. \ui{Retire} one you no longer teach (it disappears; its code is
  never reused). \ui{+ Add proficiency} for something missing.
\item \ui{Scope \& Sequence} $\to$ a strand tab. Click your course's cell in each row and write one short
  line: what your students do with that idea. Write \emph{n/a} if you don't touch it; mark optional or
  extension work as such.
\end{enumerate}
\textit{Aim: more than a bar napkin, less than a research paper.}

\section*{2. Cross-check overlaps (Round 2)}
\ui{Review} lists pairs of proficiencies in neighboring courses that look alike. With your partner,
decide each one:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Spiral}: taught again, deeper. Good; say how in the note.
\item \textbf{Redundant}: one course should drop or change it.
\item \textbf{Not the same}: similar words, different skill.
\end{itemize}
Disagree, or see a gap? \ui{+ Raise a question}; it goes on the November agenda.

\section*{3. Add resources (any time)}
On any proficiency, \ui{Add resource}: give it a title, then \textbf{paste a link} or \textbf{upload a file}
(PDF, image, doc; under 20\,MB). Pick a kind: video, practice problems, notes, interactive.
\begin{itemize}
\item Free, no student login: Khan Academy, Desmos, YouTube, your own worksheets.
\item Your own work, or openly licensed. No textbook scans.
\item Practice and review only, never a current quiz or test.
\end{itemize}
Recommending an outside site you like is as valuable as uploading: one good link per proficiency is the goal.
\end{multicols}

\vspace{-6pt}
\begin{multicols}{2}
\section*{4. Summer and make-up practice}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \ui{Assign practice}. Either pick \ui{Summer before \emph{(next course)}} to load everything
  the next course assumes, or search and tick specific proficiencies.
\item Add a title, a note to the student, and a due date.
\item \ui{Create link}: copy the \textbf{link}, show the \textbf{QR code}, or copy the \textbf{email text}.
\item Or \ui{Make a class code}: a 5-character code like \texttt{K7QM4} for the board or a report comment.
\end{enumerate}
\textbf{The student} opens the site, types the code on the home page (or opens the link), no login.
They get a checklist; each proficiency has its resources and self-check problems, new numbers every
try. The app remembers their codes. \emph{No student information is stored anywhere.}
Try the demo: code \texttt{NWA26}.

\columnbreak
\section*{5. Send Ryan your files}
Email or share with Ryan:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Old exams and quizzes, with keys}, plus worksheets and review packets.
\item \textbf{Outside resources you like}: the link, and which proficiency or unit it fits.
\end{itemize}
Exams are \emph{never posted}. Claude reads them, tags each question to proficiency codes, and reports
which proficiencies our assessments cover and which they miss. It then writes \emph{new} practice
problems in that style for each proficiency, with worked answers and fresh numbers each try. Many are
\textbf{graphical}: read a graph, or answer by \emph{doing} it: place a point, drag a curve, set a
slider, sketch on Desmos. You review your course's problems in the Workshop before they go public;
then they appear in every summer link.
\end{multicols}
